Fellowship 2026 brought together **1,072 fellows** for three months, across two paths: **Full Stack AI Engineering** and **Data Engineering**. When it ended, we asked fellows what they had built, what they had solved and where it had taken them. This report shares what they told us.

| What fellows did | Total |
| --- | --- |
| Projects built | 474 |
| LeetCode problems solved | 5,860 |

## Where it led them

**131 fellows** took at least one of these paths. Some took more than one.

| Path | Fellows |
| --- | --- |
| Secured an internship | 79 |
| Cleared interviews | 61 |
| Landed a job | 21 |
| Contributing to open source | 38 |
| Working as freelancers | 32 |

## What they built

- **E-commerce stores** were the most common build, many of them on the MERN stack.
- **Real estate apps and learning management systems**, full stack, with authentication, roles and dashboards.
- **AI features**: chatbots and assistants, RAG systems and agents, from the Full Stack AI Engineering path.
- **Data pipelines and warehouses**, from the Data Engineering path.
- **First builds** such as weather apps, task managers and portfolios, where many fellows started before moving on to bigger projects.

## Beyond the code

The fellowship was never only technical. Alongside projects and DSA, fellows logged their talks, reading and reflection every week.

| Beyond the code | Total |
| --- | --- |
| Mindset talks | 673 |
| Spiritual talks | 659 |
| Dinner engineer talks | 370 |
| Articles read | 834 |
| Pages of reflection written | 454 |
| Recorded DSA sessions watched | 837 |
| Live engineering sessions attended | 442 |

Recorded sessions were the format most fellows found effective, because they could learn at their own pace.

## How fellows rated it

| Rating | Score |
| --- | --- |
| Overall experience | 4.2 / 5 |
| Mentor support | 4.3 / 5 |

Thank you to every mentor who gave their weekends to a clan. That 4.3 is yours.
