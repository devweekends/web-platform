Our Summer Fellowship 2025 had **800 fellows**, after a first cohort of 300+ earlier that year. **100 finished and were certified**. This report covers those 100, from their own answers when the fellowship ended.

| What the certified fellows did | Total |
| --- | --- |
| Projects built | 442 (about 4.4 each) |
| LeetCode problems solved | 8,000 (about 80 each) |

## Where it led them

Of the 100 certified fellows, **59 took at least one of these paths**. Some took more than one.

| Path | Fellows |
| --- | --- |
| Landed a full-time job | 13 |
| Secured an internship | 17 |
| Cleared interviews | 16 |
| Working as freelancers | 13 |
| Contributing to open source | 16 |

## What they built

- **Real estate apps**, built by several fellows: full stack, with authentication, listings and search.
- **Multivendor e-commerce**: vendor management, payments and real-time updates.
- **Learning management systems**: course management, user roles and progress tracking.

## Beyond the code

The fellowship was never only technical. Alongside projects and DSA, fellows went through mindset talks, spiritual talks, dinner talks, weekly articles and daily reflection writing, and logged each one in their weekly reports.

Not every story was a success, and fellows said so too. One wrote that it *"didn't helped me because of my inconsistency and laziness."*
