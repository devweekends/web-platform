Dev Weekends is partnering with [Olostep](https://www.olostep.com).

Olostep turns any website into clean, structured data for AI apps and agents. You can scrape a page, crawl a whole site or search the web, all through one API. Our own [community feed](/feed) already runs on it: it finds #DevWeekends posts with the Olostep Search API.

Here is what the partnership brings to the community.

## 20,000 free credits for all of November

Our whole community of 30,000+ engineers gets 20,000 free Olostep credits for the month of November. If you have been waiting for a reason to build something with real web data, this is it.

## Lifetime free credits for DWOSI projects

Every project in [DWOSI](/dwosi), our open source initiative, gets lifetime free credits on Olostep's public and private APIs. Contributors can build features on live web data without anyone having to pay for it.

## Sponsoring our events

Olostep is sponsoring our upcoming events.

Thank you to the Olostep team for backing engineers who build.
