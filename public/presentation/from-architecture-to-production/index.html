09 / BACKPRESSURE

WHY PUT A QUEUE
IN THE MIDDLE?

A temporary traffic burst should not automatically overwhelm your workers.
300/snew jobs arriving
200/sworkers can complete
+100/squeue backlog growth
THE ENGINEERING POINT

30,000 waiting jobs after five minutes

If all 300/s are accepted into SQS, 30,000 accumulate in five minutes. Requests rejected before the queue are not saved.

ASK THE ROOMWhat happens if the burst lasts an hour?
A queue absorbs bursts; it does not create processing capacity.09 / 42
